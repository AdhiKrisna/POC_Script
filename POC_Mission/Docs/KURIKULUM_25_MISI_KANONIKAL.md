## 2. DETAIL 25 MISI KANONIKAL

### LEVEL 1: Flight Cadet (Flight School & Local Circuit)
*Bantuan UI: 4-Slot Data Card | PTT: Uncapped | 100% Rutin Murni*

1. **Misi 101: Banyuwangi (WADY) — Ground Radio Check & Taxi Routine**
   * **Pesawat / Callsign:** Cessna 172 Skyhawk | `Skyfox 12`
   * **Objektif:** Uji keterbacaan radio skala 1–5 (*Doc 9432 Ch.2.8.4*), konfirmasi ATIS, dan meminta izin taksi menuju holding point Runway 08.
   * **Uji Kritis:** Struktur 4 elemen dasar ([Stasiun] + [Callsign] + [Posisi] + [Permintaan]).

2. **Misi 102: Banyuwangi (WADY) — Standard Visual Circuit & Touch-and-Go**
   * **Pesawat / Callsign:** Cessna 172 Skyhawk | `Skyfox 24`
   * **Objektif:** Pelaporan 3 titik wajib sirkuit visual (*downwind, base, final - Doc 4444: Ch.7.7*) dan izin touch-and-go.
   * **Uji Kritis:** Menyebutkan nomor runway di akhir setiap readback izin mendarat/lepas landas secara konsisten.

3. **Misi 103: Curug (WICB) — Multi-Runway Holding Points & Stop Bar**
   * **Pesawat / Callsign:** Piper Archer PA-28 | `PK-WIC`
   * **Objektif:** Navigasi taxiway dan persimpangan landasan silang (Runway 12/30 dan 04/22) di Curug.
   * **Uji Kritis:** Kepatuhan mutlak berhenti di marka holding point dan readback izin hold short tanpa tertukar nomor landasan.

4. **Misi 104: Buleleng (WADE) — AFIS Advisory & Position Self-Announcing**
   * **Pesawat / Callsign:** Cessna 172 Skyhawk | `Indo Air 05`
   * **Objektif:** Komunikasi dua arah dengan stasiun *Buleleng Information* (AFIS), menyampaikan niat terbang (*intentions*), dan pelaporan posisi mandiri.
   * **Uji Kritis:** Self-announcing posisi visual tanpa komando radar.

5. **Misi 105: Banyuwangi (WADY) — Full Aerodrome Circuit VFR Checkride**
   * **Pesawat / Callsign:** Diamond DA40 | `PK-ABC`
   * **Objektif:** Ujian mandiri penuh 1 putaran sirkuit visual lengkap (Apron ➔ Takeoff ➔ Downwind ➔ Final Full Stop ➔ Apron).
   * **Uji Kritis:** Kelancaran otomatisasi motorik tanpa jeda ragu.

### LEVEL 2: Junior First Officer (Regional Controlled & Basic Instrument)
*Bantuan UI: Cloze Text (Teks Rumpang) | PTT: ≤ 8s | 4 Skenario Abnormal Darat/Tanjak*

1. **Misi 201: Yogyakarta YIA (WAHI) ➔ Lombok Praya (WADL)**
   * **Pesawat / Callsign:** ATR 72-600 | `Wings Air 1822`
   * **Skenario:** `[ABNORMAL]` Pushback Delay (*Doc 9432 Ch.4.3.1*)
   * **Letak Masalah:** Di apron YIA, antrean dorong mundur tertunda akibat pesawat ATR lain melintas di belakang.
   * **Dialog Kunci:** ATC: *"Wings Air 1822, hold position, expect three minutes delay due traffic behind."* ➔ Pilot: *"Hold position, expect three minutes delay, Wings Air 1822."*

2. **Misi 202: Lombok Praya (WADL) ➔ Banjarmasin (WAOO)**
   * **Pesawat / Callsign:** ATR 72-600 | `Wings Air 1840`
   * **Skenario:** `[ABNORMAL]` Stop Pushback Immediately (*Doc 9432 Ch.4.3.3*)
   * **Letak Masalah:** Saat pushback berlangsung di Lombok, ground vehicle menerobos area pergerakan.
   * **Dialog Kunci:** ATC: *"Wings Air 1840, STOP PUSHBACK IMMEDIATELY, vehicle crossing behind!"* ➔ Pilot: *"STOPPING PUSHBACK, Wings Air 1840."*

3. **Misi 203: Banjarmasin (WAOO) ➔ Palembang (WIPP)**
   * **Pesawat / Callsign:** Cessna 208B Grand Caravan | `Susi Air 112`
   * **Skenario:** `[ABNORMAL]` Unable Altitude Profile (*Doc 9432 Ch.2.6*)
   * **Letak Masalah:** ATC menginstruksikan tanjak ke FL180, melampaui kemampuan daya angkut pesawat.
   * **Dialog Kunci:** Pilot: *"Makassar Control, Susi Air 112, UNABLE Flight Level one eight zero due aircraft performance, request Flight Level one four zero."*

4. **Misi 204: Palembang (WIPP) ➔ Yogyakarta YIA (WAHI)**
   * **Pesawat / Callsign:** ATR 72-600 | `Wings Air 1890`
   * **Skenario:** `[ABNORMAL]` Hold Short Mandatory Readback (*Doc 4444: Ch.4.5.7.5b*)
   * **Letak Masalah:** Taksi menuju Runway 11 Palembang dengan penegasan batas henti.
   * **Dialog Kunci:** ATC: *"Wings Air 1890, taxi to holding point Charlie, hold short runway one one."* ➔ Pilot: *"Taxi to holding point Charlie, hold short runway one one, Wings Air 1890."*

### LEVEL 3: Senior First Officer (Busy Single-Runway & Backtrack)
*Bantuan UI: Intent Badges (Bahasa Indonesia) | PTT: ≤ 5s | 8 Skenario Taktis ICAO*

1. **Misi 301: Semarang (WARS) ➔ Bali Ngurah Rai (WADD)**
   * **Pesawat / Callsign:** Airbus A320 | `Batik 6302`
   * **Skenario:** `[ABNORMAL]` Runway Backtrack (*Doc 9432 Ch.4.4.2*)
   * **Letak Masalah:** Ahmad Yani Semarang tidak memiliki paralel taxiway; pesawat wajib putar balik 180° di landasan aktif.
   * **Dialog Kunci:** ATC: *"Batik 6302, backtrack and line up runway one three, report ready for departure."*

2. **Misi 302: Bali Ngurah Rai (WADD) ➔ Solo (WARQ)**
   * **Pesawat / Callsign:** Boeing 737-800 | `Garuda 312`
   * **Skenario:** `[ABNORMAL]` Wake Turbulence Wait (*Doc 4444: Ch.5.8*) + `[ABNORMAL]` Holding Pattern & EAT (*Doc 4444: Ch.5.5*)
   * **Letak Masalah:** Tertahan 2 menit pusaran turbulensi jet berat di Bali, lalu disuruh masuk holding antrean cuaca di Solo.

3. **Misi 303: Solo (WARQ) ➔ Balikpapan (WALL)**
   * **Pesawat / Callsign:** Airbus A320 | `Supergreen 512`
   * **Skenario:** `[ABNORMAL]` Conditional Line-Up 'Behind' (*Doc 9432 Ch.4.5.7*)
   * **Uji Kritis:** Izin bersyarat wajib mengulang kata *"BEHIND"* di awal dan di akhir readback.

4. **Misi 304: Balikpapan (WALL) ➔ Padang (WIPT)**
   * **Pesawat / Callsign:** Boeing 737-800 | `Lion Air 720`
   * **Skenario:** `[ABNORMAL]` Immediate Take-Off (*Doc 9432 Ch.4.5.5*) + `[ABNORMAL]` Delaying Vector / Orbit 360° (*Doc 9432 Ch.4.6.5*)
   * **Letak Masalah:** Lepas landas kilat tanpa henti di Balikpapan disusul manuver orbit 360° kanan di Padang untuk separasi radar.

5. **Misi 305: Padang (WIPT) ➔ Batam (WIDD)**
   * **Pesawat / Callsign:** Airbus A320 | `Batik 6350`
   * **Skenario:** `[URGENCY]` Flap Asymmetry High Speed (*Doc 9432 Ch.9*)
   * **Letak Masalah:** Flap macet di final approach Batam; pilot mendeklarasikan PAN PAN dan meminta pendaratan laju 160 knot.

6. **Misi 306: Batam (WIDD) ➔ Semarang (WARS)**
   * **Pesawat / Callsign:** Boeing 737-800 | `Garuda 380`
   * **Skenario:** `[ABNORMAL]` Continue Approach on Short Final (*Doc 9432 Ch.4.7.1*)
   * **Letak Masalah:** Di jarak 2 NM final Semarang, izin mendarat belum keluar. Pilot diinstruksikan melanjutkan approach sambil bersiap go-around.

### LEVEL 4: Captain (Metropolitan Hub & Multi-Runway)
*Bantuan UI: Cockpit HUD Strip (Audio-Only) | PTT: ≤ 3s | 8 Skenario Kompaun Hub*

1. **Misi 401: Jakarta CGK (WIII) ➔ Surabaya (WARR)**
   * **Pesawat / Callsign:** Boeing 777-300ER Heavy | `Garuda 123 Heavy`
   * **Skenario:** `[ABNORMAL]` CTOT Slot Management (*Doc 4444 Ch.3*) + `[ABNORMAL]` Dual Runway Crossing (*Doc 4444: Ch.7.6.3.2*)
   * **Uji Kritis:** Readback slot waktu lepas landas terhitung dan izin menyeberangi RWY 07L sambil menahan sebelum RWY 07R dalam satu tarikan napas.

2. **Misi 402: Surabaya (WARR) ➔ Makassar (WAAA)**
   * **Pesawat / Callsign:** Airbus A320 | `Batik 712`
   * **Skenario:** `[ABNORMAL]` Callsign Confusion Vigilance (*Doc 9432 Ch.2.7.2*) + `[DISTRESS]` High-Speed RTO (*Doc 9432 Ch.4.5.11*)
   * **Uji Kritis:** Menahan diri diam saat nomor mirip dipanggil (`Lion 712`), dan teriak *"STOPPING"* seketika saat pembatalan lepas landas di 110 knot.

3. **Misi 403: Makassar (WAAA) ➔ Medan Kualanamu (WIMM)**
   * **Pesawat / Callsign:** Airbus A330-300 Heavy | `Garuda 612 Heavy`
   * **Skenario:** `[ABNORMAL]` Strategic Lateral Offset / SLOP (*Doc 4444: Ch.16.5*) + `[DISTRESS]` TCAS Resolution Advisory (*Doc 4444: Ch.15.7.3*)
   * **Uji Kritis:** Deviasi lateral 1 NM kanan di Selat Karimata dan kepatuhan instan alarm kokpit "CLIMB" mengabaikan ATC sesaat.

4. **Misi 404: Medan Kualanamu (WIMM) ➔ Jakarta Halim (WIHH)**
   * **Pesawat / Callsign:** Airbus A320 | `Batik 7014`
   * **Skenario:** `[URGENCY]` In-Flight Medical Diversion (*Doc 4444: Ch.15.1*)
   * **Uji Kritis:** Deklarasi 3x PAN PAN untuk serangan jantung penumpang di FL330, direct descent ke Halim, dan armada ambulans siaga.

5. **Misi 405: Jakarta Halim (WIHH) ➔ Jakarta CGK (WIII)**
   * **Pesawat / Callsign:** Boeing 737-800 | `Lion Air 802`
   * **Skenario:** `[URGENCY]` Blown Tire Blocking Runway on Rollout (*Doc 4444: Ch.15.1*)
   * **Uji Kritis:** Ban utama kiri meledak saat touchdown di RWY 25L Cengkareng; melapor pesawat lumpuh (*disabled*), runway terblokir, dan meminta mobil derek (*tow tug*).

### LEVEL 5: Senior Captain (Extreme Terrain & High-Risk Valley)
*Bantuan UI: Mountain HUD + Partyline Audio Chatter | PTT: ≤ 3s | 8 Kedaruratan Mutlak*

1. **Misi 501: Wamena (WAVV) ➔ Timika (WABP)**
   * **Pesawat / Callsign:** Boeing 737-500 STOL | `Trigana 241`
   * **Skenario:** `[ABNORMAL]` Pilot-Initiated Go-Around (*Doc 9432 Ch.4.8.3*) + `[URGENCY]` Volcanic Ash Avoidance (*Doc 4444: Ch.15.8*)
   * **Uji Kritis:** Inisiatif mandiri batal mendarat saat melihat rintangan mobil teknisi di Wamena, dan deviasi darurat abu letusan vulkanik di rute pegunungan.

2. **Misi 502: Bandung (WICC) ➔ Toraja (WAFB)**
   * **Pesawat / Callsign:** ATR 72-600 | `Smart 102`
   * **Skenario:** `[DISTRESS]` GPWS Terrain Avoidance / Authority Breaker (*Doc 4444: Ch.15.7.4*)
   * **Uji Kritis:** Membantah tegas perintah keliru ATC Bandung yang mengarahkan pesawat menabrak tebing Gunung Malabar: *"UNABLE heading two one zero and descend due terrain, climbing Flight Level zero nine zero!"*.

3. **Misi 503: Toraja (WAFB) ➔ Tarakan (WALR)**
   * **Pesawat / Callsign:** ATR 72-600 | `Smart 105`
   * **Skenario:** `[URGENCY]` Severe Wind Shear Microburst Escape (*Doc 9432 Ch.11.4*)
   * **Uji Kritis:** Tenaga mesin penuh (TOGA) dan lapor instan di bibir tebing curam Toraja: *"Smart 105, WIND SHEAR ESCAPE, climbing straight ahead to six thousand feet!"*.

4. **Misi 504: Tarakan (WALR) ➔ Timika (WABP)**
   * **Pesawat / Callsign:** Boeing 737-500 STOL | `Trigana 810`
   * **Skenario:** `[DISTRESS]` Emergency Descent Decompression to FL170 (*Doc 4444: Ch.15.1.4*) + `[DISTRESS]` MAYDAY FUEL (*Doc 4444: Ch.15.5.4*)
   * **Uji Kritis:** Penurunan darurat dekompresi di Papua ke FL170 (ketinggian batas aman pegunungan Jayawijaya MORA, bukan FL100) dan deklarasi mutlak MAYDAY FUEL saat sisa 20 menit terbang.

5. **Misi 505: Timika (WABP) ➔ Wamena (WAVV)**
   * **Pesawat / Callsign:** ATR 72-600 | `Trigana 245`
   * **Skenario:** `[DISTRESS]` EFATO / Engine Out Drift-Down (*Doc 4444: Ch.15.1*) + `[DISTRESS]` Runway Excursion & ARFF (*Doc 4444: Ch.9.2*)
   * **Uji Kritis:** Mesin kanan meledak di 400 kaki AGL lepas landas Timika, air-turn-back darurat, pendaratan 1 mesin disusul pesawat tergelincir ke rumput (*runway excursion*) dan aktivasi armada busa pemadam ARFF.
